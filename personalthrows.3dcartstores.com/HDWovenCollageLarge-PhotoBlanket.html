    Q: Do any of your blankets allow for 20+ pictures? Ideally, I'm looking to put a large photo/design in the middle and have children's artwork in smaller photos around this
    Asked by: Alicia - 9/24/2014
    A: 

    We do offer the option of using 20+ pictures by default. The limit on the number of images allowed on this collage correspond to how the detail is best preserved when sublimating it on the blanket – the texture of the woven fabric commonly causes loss of much finer details on the image.

    While this isn't an issue with most collage or single images, the more images put in a collage, the finer the details of the final image will be – which means there's a higher likelihood of detail on the images being lost in the blanket's texture.


    We cannot advise 20+ images on collage for this type of blanket for this reason, although the risk of detail loss is lowered if they are single-subject/non-complex images. An additional art fee is also incurred for collages using more than 12 images (based on the number of images added to that amount), which would mean this would need to be set up as a custom order.


    If you are still interested going forward with a 20+ image collage, then please call our Customer Service department at 866.678.6933, Call Us 9am to 5pm EST, Monday-Friday.

    Answer provided by:  Customer Service (9/26/2014)

    Image File Size Guidelines

    Your file should meet these minimum requirements, without large areas of white around the image. Small web images/thumbnails cannot be accepted.

    Minimum size needed - 4.7 Megapixels

    • File Size as JPEG/JPG - Minimum of 672 KB
    • File Size as PNG - Minimum of 5.45 MB
    • Other file types, such as PSD, AI, PDF, and EPS are acceptable so long as they meet the minimum in megapixels.

     

    DOs & DO NOTs Quick Guide

    DO

    • Try to upload the original file from the device it was acquired on.
    • Check your image's file size before submitting it.
    • Use a scanner to digitize hard copy photos and artwork.
    • Try to send us the highest resolution version of your image available.
    • Contact Us if you are unsure if your file(s) meets our requirements.

    DO NOT

    • Upload files that you have saved directly off of Facebook, Instagram, or other web sources.
    • Take a picture of a hard copy photo or your computer monitor with your phone or camera to acquire an image.
    • Submit a screenshot of your smartphone's photo gallery instead of the actual photo file.
    • Place your order if you're unsure if your file(s) meets our requirements.
