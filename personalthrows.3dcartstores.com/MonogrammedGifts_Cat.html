    Monogrammed Gifts

    Personal Throws has always been your source for the highest quality photo blankets and other imagery-based gift ideas, but now we also offer an amazingly stylish line of monogrammed gifts as well. When a full photo is a little too bold, a monogrammed gift like a beach towel or gallery wrap is the perfect way to make it personal and still qualify as an understated aesthetic.

    We offer three beautiful designs to choose from, including the Chevron, Nautical and Blooming Spring motifs, and product lines like monogrammed mouse pads, cooking aprons. fleece blankets and more. Artistic and elegant, these make perfect gifts for anyone who appreciates a little elegance in their daily world. Available in a rainbow of color patterns, simply choose the design and the product line below, and let us know the monogram details you'd like. At Personal Throws, we let you add the personalized touch in every gift you give!
