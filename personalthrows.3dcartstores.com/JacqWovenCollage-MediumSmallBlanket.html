    You want to Put more than one Photo on your throw? We understand and provide you your solution. The COLLAGE. We know that sometimes it is hard to associate just one photograph with your favorite memorable moments. You probably want to capture every bit of it. We offer you a perfect product to capture your memories for ever. All you have to do is provide us your favorite pictures and we will do all the work. We will create your collage layout and then weave it into a beautiful throw that will remind you of your memorable moments in life. Please note that since we are going to use more pictures in the same size throw, it is more important to have really high resolution pictures with good contrast and not too many subjects in individual pictures. We recommend that you choose up to 4 of your favorite photographs to make a collage. Please feel free to contact us if you have any questions about the images or otherwise.

    Production Time:Usually Ships in 2-3 weeks.

    The size indicated includes the fringes. The dimensions do not mean the image itself will be that exact size and additionally there is a 5-7% variance in size due to woven nature of product and cotton shrinkage factors.

    • 54" x 38" (Small) 
    • Image is converted into a 6-color loom pattern and woven into a blanket from over 8 mile of cotton yarn.
    • 100% Egyptian Cotton
    • Use up to 4 Images (Single Subject Photos Only HIGHLY recommended)
    • Ships in 3-4 Weeks.
    • Jacquard woven blankets have more limited color palette than HD wovens because the threads are carefully dyed and woven instead of printed.
    • Texture: Traditional soft woven feel with a heavier weight than polar and plush fleece or HD wovens
    Tips for Ordering: If you want your order to be processed even faster, here are some ways you can help us! 
    • Always include an in hand date if there is one, no matter how tentative. If you are curious as to if we can handle your in hand date, just give us a call!
    • Check your email, especially if you have purchased a proof. This way, our art team can communicate quickly with you, such as if there are questions or issues.
    • Double check your photo before uploading to make sure it is a good image! If it is an image that cannot be optimized, it will take more time for our art team to request and receive an alternative image. If you are unsure, call or Click Here to review our guidelines!
    • Place your order during business hours. Our hours are from 9AM-5PM EST, Monday through Friday. If an order gets placed after we are closed, we are unable to view it until the next day. Same goes for ordering on the weekend! If every minute counts, it's best to place it during the day when our customer service can resolve any image issues, answer questions, etc.

    Image File Size Guidelines

    Your file should meet these minimum requirements, without large areas of white around the image. Small web images/thumbnails cannot be accepted.

    Minimum size needed - 4.7 Megapixels

    • File Size as JPEG/JPG - Minimum of 672 KB
    • File Size as PNG - Minimum of 5.45 MB
    • Other file types, such as PSD, AI, PDF, and EPS are acceptable so long as they meet the minimum in megapixels.

     

    DOs & DO NOTs Quick Guide

    DO

    • Try to upload the original file from the device it was acquired on.
    • Check your image's file size before submitting it.
    • Use a scanner to digitize hard copy photos and artwork.
    • Try to send us the highest resolution version of your image available.
    • Contact Us if you are unsure if your file(s) meets our requirements.

    DO NOT

    • Upload files that you have saved directly off of Facebook, Instagram, or other web sources.
    • Take a picture of a hard copy photo or your computer monitor with your phone or camera to acquire an image.
    • Submit a screenshot of your smartphone's photo gallery instead of the actual photo file.
    • Place your order if you're unsure if your file(s) meets our requirements.
