    The Medium Jacquard COLLAGE: We know that sometimes it is hard to associate just one photograph with your favorite memorable moments. You probably want to capture every bit of it, so all you have to do is provide us your favorite pictures and we will do all the work. We will create your collage layout and then weave it into a beautiful throw that will remind you of your memorable moments in life. The medium is the more cost effective option out of the loom-woven collages. We recommend that you choose 4-6 of your favorite photographs to make a collage. Please feel free to contact us if you have any questions about the images or otherwise.

    The size indicated includes the fringes. The dimensions do not mean the image itself will be that exact size and additionally there is a 5-7% variance in size due to woven nature of product and cotton shrinkage factors.

    • Size: 60" x 54" (Medium)
    • Process: Image is converted into a 6-color loom pattern and woven into a blanket from over 8 mile of cotton yarn.
    • Material: 100% Egyptian Cotton
    • # of Images: Use up to 6 Images (Single Subject Photos Only HIGHLY recommended)
    • Production Time: Ships in 3-4 Weeks.
    • Jacquard woven blankets have more limited color palette than HD wovens because the threads are carefully dyed and woven instead of printed.
    • Texture: Traditional soft woven feel with a heavier weight than polar and plush fleece or HD wovens.

    Q: We may have up to 3 people in a picture...will that come out clear? and why is it highly recommended that only one image is in the picture?
    Asked by: JILL - 7/11/2015
    A: 

    So long as the image of the three people is clear, and not very zoomed out, then it should come out clear – however that can only be guaranteed if you're using if for our single-image version of the blanket. To clarify we recommend that only single subject images be submitted for the Jacquard woven collages because the processed used to produce them is limited in the amount of image detail that can be carried over.

    To create these blankets, a digital image is converted into a 6-color thread loom pattern to be woven into the blanket itself. In order to keep the blanket's weave at the right density to still feel like a blanket, the pattern cannot carry over very intricate details.

    It is for this reason that we recommend only single subject photos and/or images for Jacquard woven collages. The images must be sized down to be arranged within a preset layout size, meaning more detail is lost when creating the pattern. While this isn't problematic for collages using single subject photos, a collage consisting of several group photos would result in details being lost in the final pattern such as: people's faces in group photos or zoomed out shots, details of buildings or surroundings, and so forth.

    Having experienced these issues when we began offering the Jacquard blankets, decided to put those requirements in place as to prevent them and likewise to prevent a product that our customers would be dissatisfied with from being produced.

    Q: what is the difference in jacquard and hd? I want to order a quilt for my parents and love the looks of yours. Thank you
    Asked by: Cindy Haney - 11/19/2013
    A: The Jacquard is created using a process that turns your photo into a loom pattern and is them spun into a blanket. HD wovens are the result of dying the image onto a blank woven blanket using the same process used for our polar and plush fleece blankets.

    While Jacquards add a rustic effect to the photo (which can be nice for a personalized heirloom), HDs tend to be better for color matching and preserving smaller details from the original image. Answer provided by:  Customer Service (1/3/2014)

    Image File Size Guidelines

    Your file should meet these minimum requirements, without large areas of white around the image. Small web images/thumbnails cannot be accepted.

    Minimum size needed - 4.7 Megapixels

    • File Size as JPEG/JPG - Minimum of 672 KB
    • File Size as PNG - Minimum of 5.45 MB
    • Other file types, such as PSD, AI, PDF, and EPS are acceptable so long as they meet the minimum in megapixels.

     

    DOs & DO NOTs Quick Guide

    DO

    • Try to upload the original file from the device it was acquired on.
    • Check your image's file size before submitting it.
    • Use a scanner to digitize hard copy photos and artwork.
    • Try to send us the highest resolution version of your image available.
    • Contact Us if you are unsure if your file(s) meets our requirements.

    DO NOT

    • Upload files that you have saved directly off of Facebook, Instagram, or other web sources.
    • Take a picture of a hard copy photo or your computer monitor with your phone or camera to acquire an image.
    • Submit a screenshot of your smartphone's photo gallery instead of the actual photo file.
    • Place your order if you're unsure if your file(s) meets our requirements.
