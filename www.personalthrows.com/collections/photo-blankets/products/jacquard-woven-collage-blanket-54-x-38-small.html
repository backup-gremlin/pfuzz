Jacquard Woven Quick Collage Blanket-4 Image Template- 54" x 38" (Small)

No reviews
  • Sale
  • $84.99
  • Regular price $129.95

A traditional woven blanket created by turning your image into a loom pattern. Three-layer woven, 100% cotton. An instant heirloom! This option allows for you to quickly and easily set up a 4 image collage using our template and your photos!

The size indicated includes the fringes. The dimensions do not mean the image itself will be that exact size and additionally there is a 5-7% variance in size due to woven nature of product and cotton shrinkage factors.

  • Size: 54" x 38"
  • Material: 100% cotton
  • Production Process: Jacquard Weaving with 100% Cotton Yarn
  • Production Time: Ships in 3-4 Weeks.
  • Image is converted to a 6-color pattern and woven into a blanket. Best suited for closeup images.

NOTE: The "Collage Border" area of any quick collage layout must be filled with a background color or pattern fill of some kind and cannot be left empty. Please do not place text or photos in the "Collage Border" area.
